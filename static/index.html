rules / help

Supported and unsupported platforms

Support follows the public yt-dlp extractors. Login requirements, private posts, geo-blocks, rate limits, or platform changes may cause a URL to fail.

✓ Supported to try

YouTube video/Shorts and audio.
TikTok video and carousel/photo posts with multi-photo selection.
Telegram public posts with direct media.
Pinterest publicly accessible photo/video pins.
Other platforms supported by yt-dlp will be tried automatically.

× Not supported

Instagram is intentionally disabled because it usually requires cookies/login.
Spotify cannot be extracted because of DRM.
Apple Music, Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Max, Tidal, Deezer, Crunchyroll are also blocked because of DRM/login.

Tips for better results

Paste a single post URL, not a playlist or radio URL. For TikTok carousels, wait for the preview to show the photo count, then select the photos you want. Make sure FFmpeg is installed to merge video and audio or convert audio.
